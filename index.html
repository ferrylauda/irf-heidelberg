About us

A home for believers from every nation

The International Reformed Fellowship in Heidelberg is a community of Christians – students, families and professionals – from many countries who have made Heidelberg their home, whether for a season or for a lifetime.

We gather to hear God’s Word faithfully preached, to worship Him in spirit and in truth, and to care for one another in love. Our faith stands in the tradition of the Protestant Reformation – a heritage closely tied to this very city, where the Heidelberg Catechism was written in 1563.

Whether you have followed Christ for many years, are new to the faith, or are simply curious – you are warmly welcome.

What we believe

Our Confession

We confess the historic Christian faith as summarised in the ecumenical creeds and the Reformed confessions. We do not place them above Scripture; we hold them because they faithfully summarise what Scripture teaches.

Ecumenical Creeds

With the Church of all ages we confess:

  • Apostles’ Creed
  • Nicene Creed (325 / 381)
  • Athanasian Creed

Three Forms of Unity

Together with Reformed churches worldwide, we hold to:

  • Belgic Confession (1561)
    A clear summary of the doctrines of the Christian faith.
  • Heidelberg Catechism (1563)
    Written here in Heidelberg – a warm, personal teaching of the gospel in 129 questions and answers.
  • Canons of Dort (1618–1619)
    The Reformed teaching on God’s sovereign grace in salvation.

The Five Solas of the Reformation

  • Sola ScripturaScripture alone
  • Sola GratiaGrace alone
  • Sola FideFaith alone
  • Solus ChristusChrist alone
  • Soli Deo GloriaTo the glory of God alone
