Privacy Policy

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and does not load fonts or scripts from third parties.

Hosting: This website is hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS). When you visit the site, technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access may be processed in server logs in order to deliver the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Map: The map on our homepage is only loaded from OpenStreetMap after you click “Show map”. In that case your IP address is transmitted to the OpenStreetMap Foundation.

Language setting: Your chosen language is stored only locally in your browser (localStorage) and is never sent to us.

Email: If you contact us by email, we use your data only to answer your enquiry and do not pass it on to third parties.

You have the right to information, correction, deletion and restriction of processing of your data, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Please contact us at the address above.
